## On Mistakes

If you discover you have taken a wrong or forbidden action (created duplicates, skipped a required step, used a prohibited command, jumped ahead without verifying state), **STOP immediately. Do not attempt to self-correct.**

Announce:
1. What happened (specific action taken)
2. Why it was wrong (which rule or step was violated)
3. Two or three concrete options for resolution

Then wait for explicit user choice before taking any further action. "Fixing it quickly" is not a valid reason to skip this step.
