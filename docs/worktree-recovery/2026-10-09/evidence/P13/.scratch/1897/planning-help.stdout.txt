/task plan-approve — Record Plan approval with durable human or Full-Auto provenance, or reconstruct skipped Full-Auto provenance from strict later-stage evidence.

  Purpose: Record Plan approval with durable human or Full-Auto provenance, or reconstruct skipped Full-Auto provenance from strict later-stage evidence.
  Usage:  /task plan-approve #N [--repair-from-evidence]

  Arguments:
    #N
        Required issue number whose plan approval is recorded.
    --repair-from-evidence
        Develop/Test/Review only: reconstruct Full-Auto approval after a revoked approval.plan exception

  Preconditions:
    Ordinary approval requires Plan; explicit evidence repair requires Develop, Test, or Review, TT_FULL_AUTO=1, complete planning/lifecycle evidence, a revoked exception chain that covered approval.plan, and GitHub edit history proving no semantic scope drift.

  Effects:
    Records the human or Full-Auto plan-approval marker used by the Plan-to-Develop gate; evidence repair additionally posts a canonical audit naming its revoked exception record.

  Output:
    Prints approval provenance or a prompt explaining the missing approval prerequisite.

  Exit codes:
    0  success (or idempotent no-op)
    1  runtime error (message on stderr)
    2  usage error / unknown verb
    3  issue is not in Plan for ordinary approval
    7  target differs from the active issue binding
    9  live board state differs from the recorded state marker
    10  issue assignee is mismatched, absent, or unverifiable
    12  plan evidence preflight failed
    14  linked plan violates governed issue-record or scratch policy
    15  evidence-derived Full-Auto repair predicates were not satisfied

  Examples:
    /task plan-approve 667
    TT_FULL_AUTO=1 /task plan-approve 667 --repair-from-evidence

  Related:
    plan-estimate
    promote
