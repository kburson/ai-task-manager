Replace participant-side, model-visible `co-review wait` loops with a context-efficient wake process that preserves the filesystem protocol as durable role authority.

Introduce a repository/host wake coordinator that observes validated protocol revisions and wakes the configured participant only for an actionable change: its role becomes available, the protocol becomes terminal, integrity fails, or human intervention is required. Idle observation must execute outside participant chat sessions and must not append unchanged polls, full state snapshots, or progress chatter to their transcripts.

A role handoff remains durable only after the existing mutex-protected event and atomic state update complete. Wake delivery is derivative and at-least-once: key it by protocol ID, protocol revision, target role, and configured provider/session identity so retries are idempotent and duplicate wakes are harmless. A restart, missed filesystem notification, chat compaction, or crash between the role swap and notification must be recoverable through an out-of-context reconciliation scan; no participant may remain indefinitely dormant after authority has passed to it.

Wake decisions must use validated `currentRole`, `turnState`, lifecycle, integrity, and provider/session authority. The absence of `.co-review-lock` is never evidence that a participant owns the turn; the lock remains only the short-lived mutation serializer and surviving-lock evidence is never stolen. Use filesystem change notification for prompt delivery where supported, plus a low-frequency external timer as the missed-event recovery path.

Deliver only a bounded pointer capsule into the participant session: protocol/runtime identity, expected revision, actor, reason for wake, and exact next command. Peer review prose and repeated protocol state stay in immutable runtime files and are read on demand. Providers that cannot durably wake a dormant session must fail capability negotiation visibly or declare a manual fallback; they must not advertise autonomous co-review and silently leave a reviewer in a coma.

Preserve the current claim, handoff, acceptance, intervention, archive, and integrity semantics. Coordinate terminal author wake/finalization with #1516 and keep #1377's broader phased-review work separate. Begin design and implementation only after the currently active #1219 co-review process reaches its intended terminal checkpoint.
