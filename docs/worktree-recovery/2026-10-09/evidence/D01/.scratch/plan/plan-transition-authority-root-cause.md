The observed system-level cause is a split between transition authorization and persisted provenance. The Plan-to-Develop gate can accept `approval.plan` as waived from the current workflow-exception resolver, but the successful transition does not persist a transition-bound typed authority record. Later consumers re-evaluate the current exception head or search for an approval marker. When the exception is revoked, neither source represents the historical authorization that allowed the transition.

The implementation must trace and confirm the exact writer/call path before choosing a fix. In particular, determine whether the missing record is an intentional consequence of treating waivers as non-approval, an omitted persistence step after policy evaluation, or a mismatch between the transition audit schema and downstream Plan-approval readers.
