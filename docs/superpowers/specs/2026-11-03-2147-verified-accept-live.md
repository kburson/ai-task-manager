## Audit and recovery contract

The reconciliation audit is best-effort evidence after verified primary repair. A verified repair may exit 0 even if the audit is absent. Attempt the audit at most once per invocation, without a transport retry that could duplicate an ambiguously acknowledged append. If that attempt fails or its acknowledgement is lost, independently verify the primary body evidence and board again before publishing the cache and returning success. An observed conflicting state, visit or demotion proof during the audit attempt remains a conflict even if a later read appears restored. An unavailable read or such a conflict returns the partial result and leaves the prior cache unchanged. An already-landed audit cannot be rolled back safely and remains observable.

Here, retry means a transport attempt within one invocation. A later operator invocation is a new decision against current evidence, not a replay of a durable repair transaction. No persistent operation token or cross-invocation audit replay is introduced. Re-running accept-live after a partial result may find no drift and retain the existing no-drift refusal; that does not mean the earlier write was undone.

The supported continuation after a partial result is to inspect the authoritative issue body, visit history and live board, retaining the partial diagnostic. If reads remain unavailable or evidence is ambiguous or inconsistent, stop mutation and resolve that condition before continuing. If the unique recorded state and valid history agree with the board, pause the current timer using `npx --no-install aitm pause`, then use ordinary `npx --no-install aitm start <N> --role agent` in the same physical worktree to re-read the issue body and refresh the derived cache. Check the Start result and any cache-seeding warning, inspect `npx --no-install aitm status`, and query `npx --no-install aitm explain <N> --json` before the next lifecycle action. Start's cache refresh is not a new reconciliation receipt and must not append a stage visit or reconciliation audit. A fresh conflict discovered by any step still blocks continuation.
